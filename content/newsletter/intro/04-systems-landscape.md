Ask a few people in your business to draw your factory's systems, and how they connect, and you may well get a different picture from each of them.

Most manufacturers have never seen their systems on one page. The systems landscape builder on the site does it in about five minutes.

## How it works

Pick the systems you run: ERP, MES/MOM, quality, maintenance, warehouse, planning and the rest. Say how each one is connected, and add anything that isn't listed. The map redraws as you go.

Reading it is simple. Orange lines go through ERP and cyan lines go straight to MOM. A dashed box is a system that isn't connected to anything.

Underneath the map you'll find what I'd look at first in a landscape like yours, such as systems doing the same job twice, or islands nobody has connected.

It's free, there's no sign-up, and everything stays in your browser. You can download the picture or copy a link to share it.

## How to get the most from it

Do it yourself first. Then ask your IT/OT lead and a production manager to do the same, without showing them yours.

Where your maps differ is worth a conversation. It's often where data gets re-keyed, or where one person quietly holds the process together.

Then take the picture to your next steering meeting. It's a conversation starter, not an architecture document, and it gets everyone looking at the same thing.
