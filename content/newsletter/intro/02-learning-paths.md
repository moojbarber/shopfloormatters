There are 97 articles on Shopfloor Matters. Nobody should read them in date order.

So I've grouped them into 13 learning paths. Each one is a set of articles in the order that makes sense, from the first idea to the last, with the total reading time shown up front. They all start from the same place: choose where you want to get to, and follow the line.

## Which one to start with

It depends on what you're trying to do:

- **New to MES/MOM?** Start with **MES/MOM foundations**: 11 articles, about 50 minutes. It covers what MES and MOM do, and how they fit alongside ERP and ISA-95.
- **Working out how your systems fit together?** **The systems landscape**: 10 articles, about 46 minutes, from integration patterns to who owns the BOM.
- **Thinking about AI on the shopfloor?** **Shopfloor AI**: 7 articles, about 28 minutes, on what AI needs from your data and where it already pays off.
- **Need to get a project approved?** **The business case**: 6 articles, about 22 minutes, from where the money comes from to choosing without regret.
- **About to start a project?** **The implementation playbook**: 10 articles, about 35 minutes, in the order you'll hit each stage.

The others go deeper into specific areas: MES functionality, metrics, quality and traceability, maintenance, connected worker, the systems around ERP, connecting the plant, and smart factory strategy.

## How they work

Mark each article as read and the path remembers where you are, so you can pick up where you left off next time. Your progress stays in your browser. When you reach the end of a path, it suggests where to go next.

These emails follow the same paths, starting with the foundations. If you read ahead on the site, you'll simply be one step ahead of your inbox.
